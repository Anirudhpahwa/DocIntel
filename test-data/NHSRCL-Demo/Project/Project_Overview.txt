NHSRCL Project Overview

The Ahmedabad-Mumbai high-speed rail corridor spans approximately 508 kilometers, connecting Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Surat, and Mumbai.

The project includes 12 stations along the corridor. Construction began in 2017, with the initial operational segment targeted for 2027.

Design speed for the corridor is 320 km/h, with a scheduled operating speed of 280 km/h for regular passenger services.
